# CRM Module

## Lead Pipeline Stages

- New Lead
- Contacted
- Follow Up
- Site Visit
- Negotiation
- Booked
- Closed
- Lost

## CRM Features

- Notes
- Follow-ups
- Calls
- WhatsApp logs
- Email logs
- Site visit scheduling
- Reminder notifications
- Lead activity timeline
- Lead reassignment
- Lost reason tracking
- Conversion reporting

## Automation Rules

- Auto-create CRM activity when a lead is generated
- Trigger WhatsApp acknowledgment after lead capture
- Trigger email with property details when requested
- Notify assigned agent immediately
- Escalate stale leads to manager after configured time

## Reporting

- Leads by stage
- Leads by source
- Agent-wise follow-up performance
- Conversion ratio
- Average closure time
