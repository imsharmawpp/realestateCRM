# Property Booking

## Booking Flow

1. Property selection
2. Customer registration or login
3. Booking amount entry
4. KYC document collection
5. Agreement upload
6. Payment initiation
7. Payment verification
8. Booking confirmation
9. Receipt and invoice generation
10. CRM stage update

## Documents

- KYC
- PAN
- Aadhar
- Agreement PDF
- Payment receipt
- Invoice

## Booking Statuses

- Draft
- Pending Payment
- Payment Failed
- Confirmed
- Cancelled
- Refunded
- Completed

## Business Rules

- Prevent double booking when a unit is reserved
- Release expired reservations after configured time
- Notify admin and agent on successful booking
- Log all booking status changes
